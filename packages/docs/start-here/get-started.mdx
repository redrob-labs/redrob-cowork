<Note>
**Redrob Cowork is free and runs entirely on your machine.** Download the desktop app, paste a Redrob Key from [console.redrob.ai](https://console.redrob.ai), and start working on your own files. There is no account to create, no organization to join, and nothing to sign in to.
</Note>

Start with the [Redrob Cowork app](https://redrob.io/download). It is a desktop app plus a
local `redrob-server`, running the Redrob Code engine as a sidecar. Your files, your
skills, your MCP servers, and your model credentials all stay on your computer.
