## Prompt

आप Kestrel Pay mobile app के नए savings feature "Goals" के PM हैं। Kestrel Pay मुंबई की एक fintech है; यह feature एक partner bank, Vasundhara Bank, के साथ बना है, जो deposits रखता है। गुरुवार की steering committee में आपको 15 मिनट मिले हैं: जॉन स्टाइल्स (COO), मैरी मेजर (CFO) और रिचर्ड माइल्स (Head of Compliance)। नीचे दिए status inputs से deck बनाइए। Team lead जेन डो ने project को **overall green** rate किया है।

## A strong answer

- Recomputes the forecast at completion as about ₹5.20 crore (₹3.64 crore + 3 remaining months × ₹52 lakh). Flags that this conflicts with the reported ₹4.72 crore and is roughly ₹40 lakh (about 8%) over the ₹4.80 crore budget.
- Catches the launch conflict: the compliance pack is submitted 14 Nov, and with Vasundhara Bank's 4-week review the earliest launch is about 12 Dec, after the 1 Dec date. The compliance and launch milestones should not be green.
- Reports the beta honestly: goal creation at 31% is below the 40% target and tickets at 22 per 1,000 exceed the limit of 15. Positives (₹2,300 first deposit vs ₹2,000 target, retention) are presented without spin, and the ticket themes (UPI round-up setting, deposit delay) are linked to fixes.
- Ends with a clear decision request with options (for example, move the launch to mid-December or January, or a soft launch to a limited audience if Vasundhara Bank allows it, plus budget approval or descoping) and a justified recommendation.
- Keeps to 6–8 slides with a speaker note on each. The deck reads at executive level with the bottom line first, and the note to Jane is factual and non-blaming.
