## Prompt

आप एक senior associate हैं। Juniper Holdings Pvt. Ltd. के general counsel ने एक वस्तुनिष्ठ legal memo माँगा है: क्या Juniper, Tessaline Packaging Pvt. Ltd. के sellers से GST demand की राशि वसूल सकता है? Juniper ने Tessaline को भारतीय क़ानून से शासित Share Purchase Agreement (SPA) के तहत ख़रीदा था; विवाद मुंबई-seated arbitration (Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996) से सुलझाए जाते हैं। आज 17 नवंबर 2025 है।

**तथ्य**

- Closing Date: 30 अप्रैल 2021। Purchase price ₹400 crore; ₹40 crore का escrow, 30 अक्टूबर 2022 को पूरा release।
- Sellers: Tessaline Founders LLP (70% Pro Rata Share) और मैरी मेजर (30%)।
- 4 अगस्त 2025: Tessaline को CGST Act की धारा 74 के तहत demand order मिला, FY2019-20 से FY2022-23 के लिए कुल ₹11.20 crore tax: FY2019-20 ₹2.60 crore; FY2020-21 ₹2.90 crore; FY2021-22 ₹3.20 crore; FY2022-23 ₹2.50 crore। Officer के workpaper में FY2021-22 को अप्रैल 2021 ₹0.40 crore और मई 2021–मार्च 2022 ₹2.80 crore में बाँटा गया है। Order के विरुद्ध appeal (धारा 107) communication से 3 महीने में दायर करनी होती है, और पर्याप्त कारण होने पर 1 महीना और।
- 3 नवंबर 2025: Juniper ने sellers को claim notice भेजा, जिसमें "₹11.20 crore, पूरी demand" माँगी गई।
- 10 नवंबर 2025: sellers के advocates का जवाब: "दावा time-barred है। Tax representation 30 अप्रैल 2024 को समाप्त हो गया, और Limitation Act की तीन साल की अवधि बीत चुकी है। आपने 30 दिन की notice की शर्त भी पूरी नहीं की।"

**SPA के अंश**

> **3.14 Taxes.** All Taxes due and owing by the Company for Pre-Closing Tax Periods have been timely paid.
>
> **6.9 Straddle Periods.** For any Straddle Period, GST and other indirect Taxes are allocated to the pre-closing portion on a closing-of-the-books basis as of the end of the Closing Date.
>
> **8.1 Survival.** The representations and warranties survive the Closing until 18 months after the Closing Date, except that Section 3.14 survives until 36 months after the Closing Date. Claims for breach of a representation must be asserted in writing before the applicable survival period expires.
>
> **8.2 Indemnification by Sellers.** Sellers shall indemnify Buyer for: (a) any breach of a representation or warranty; and (c) any and all Pre-Closing Taxes, regardless of any disclosure or of Buyer's knowledge.
>
> **8.4 Limitations.** Claims under 8.2(a) are subject to a Deductible of ₹2.5 crore and a cap equal to the Escrow Amount. Claims under 8.2(c) are not subject to the Deductible or the cap, but may not exceed the Purchase Price. Sellers' liability is several, not joint, in proportion to their Pro Rata Shares.
>
> **8.6 Tax Indemnity Survival.** The obligations under Section 8.2(c) survive until 60 days after the expiration of the applicable limitation period, but in no event longer than seven (7) years after the Closing Date.
>
> **8.7 Third-Party Claims.** Buyer shall notify Sellers of any Third-Party Claim (including any Tax demand) within 30 days after receipt. Failure to do so does not relieve Sellers of their obligations except to the extent they are actually prejudiced.
>
> **"Pre-Closing Taxes"** means Taxes for any Pre-Closing Tax Period and the pre-closing portion of any Straddle Period. **"Straddle Period"** means any taxable or assessment period that begins on or before and ends after the Closing Date.

**क़ानून (संक्षिप्त)**

>
> Indian Contract Act, 1872, धारा 124: "A contract by which one party promises to save the other from loss caused to him by the conduct of the promisor himself, or by the conduct of any other person, is called a 'contract of indemnity'."

## A strong answer

- Distinguishes the §3.14 representation claim under 8.2(a) from the independent Pre-Closing Tax indemnity in 8.2(c). The representation claim is lost: the survival period ended 30 April 2024 and, independently, Article 55 runs three years from the breach at closing (30 April 2021), so section 28 cannot rescue it. The indemnity claim accrued only when the liability became absolute with the 4 August 2025 demand, so the three-year period runs to about August 2028, and 8.6's outer limit (30 April 2028) is not reached. Concludes the claim is timely under 8.2(c); notes that 8.6 cannot extend statutory limitation and that any attempt to cut rights short could be attacked under section 28(b).
- Addresses the notice defence. Notice came 91 days after the 4 August demand, so the 30-day deadline was missed. Under 8.7 that defeats the claim only to the extent the sellers were actually prejudiced, and the memo says what prejudice they might argue: the three-month appeal window under section 107 closed on about 4 November, a day after notice, leaving only the one-month condonable extension (to about 4 December) and no chance to shape the appeal.
- Computes the recoverable amount with the closing-of-the-books allocation: ₹2.60 crore + ₹2.90 crore + ₹0.40 crore = ₹5.90 crore (not a one-month pro-rata ₹26.67 lakh for April 2021). Excludes the post-closing May 2021–March 2022 and FY2022-23 tax (₹5.30 crore), which is Juniper's own liability.
- Notes that the 8.2(c) claim isn't subject to the Deductible, the Escrow cap or the released escrow. Splits the liability severally: ₹4.13 crore (Founders LLP) and ₹1.77 crore (Mary Major).
- Recommends withdrawing or amending the ₹11.20 crore notice so it claims under 8.2(c) for ₹5.90 crore, plus related interest and penalties attributable to those periods if documented, and preserving the appeal immediately (filing within the condonable period with a condonation application and the pre-deposit).
