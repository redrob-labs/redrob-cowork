## A strong answer

- Finds three deviations Richard missed: item 2 (5.33% price variance, ₹25,600 over a ₹4,80,000 PO, more than 5%, with no re-approval), item 5 (approved 15-06, after payment on 10-06) and item 7 (₹2,96,400 approved by the Ops Manager instead of a Director). Items 4 and 8 are correctly exempt.
- Notes the sample is undersized (10 tested against the 25 required under the firm's SA 530 table) and that 3 deviations in 10 already exceed the 10% tolerable rate, so extending the sample cannot rescue reliance.
- Identifies the Ujjwal Chemicals invoices UK-7731 and UK-7732 (₹19,76,000 combined, same goods, same day, same approval) as a likely split to avoid CFO approval, and recommends follow-up, such as a duplicate or split-invoice scan of the population.
