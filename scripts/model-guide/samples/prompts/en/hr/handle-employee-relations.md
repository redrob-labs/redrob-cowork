## Prompt

You are the employee relations lead at Harbor Point Medical Supply's distribution center in Columbus, Ohio. You ran the investigation summarized below. Write the investigation report for the Director of Operations and in-house counsel. Today is October 6, 2025.

**Complaint (email from Mary Major, picker, night shift, to HR, Sept 22, 2025, 11:15 a.m.)**

> I want to report my supervisor, Richard Roe. On Sept 10 in the break room he said I "should wear tighter scrubs, it'd help morale." At the team dinner on the Sept 18 offsite he kept putting his arm around me and rubbing my back. I moved seats twice and he followed me. I'm afraid this will affect my job.

**Timeline and records**

| Date / time | Record |
|---|---|
| Sept 18, 8:42 p.m. | Restaurant receipt on Richard Roe's expense report: "Team dinner, 9 attendees", $412.60, submitted by Roe Sept 19 |
| Sept 19, 7:02 a.m. | Slack DM, Roe to Major: "hope last night didn't make things weird lol" (no reply) |
| Sept 19, 3:30 p.m. | Draft schedule-change form created by scheduler Jane Doe: "Coverage rebalancing — move 3 pickers (Major, P. Lin, D. Ortiz) from Tue–Sat nights to Fri–Mon nights, effective Oct 5" |
| Sept 22, 4:00 p.m. | HR notifies Roe of the complaint and gives him the anti-retaliation reminder |
| Sept 23, 9:10 a.m. | Form edited by Roe: Lin and Ortiz removed; only Major moved. Approved by Roe at 9:14 a.m. |
| Sept 24 | Major tells HR the weekend shift conflicts with her childcare |

**Interview notes (summaries)**

- **Mary Major:** Repeats the complaint. Says that on Sept 10 she told her coworker John Stiles what Roe had said. She has no other witnesses to the break-room remark.
- **Richard Roe:** Denies both allegations. On Sept 18: "I wasn't at the dinner. I drove home after the afternoon session." On the schedule: "Lin and Ortiz had seniority, so I kept them. It's an operational call." He denies knowing about the complaint when he edited the form.
- **John Stiles (picker):** Was at the dinner. Saw Roe "put his arm around Mary a couple of times" and saw her change seats. Didn't hear what was said. Says that around Sept 10 Mary told him Roe had "said something gross about her scrubs."
- **Richard Miles (picker):** Not at the dinner. Says "everyone knows Richard talks like that," and that he heard it from a coworker he won't name.
- **Jane Doe (scheduler):** Created the three-person draft because Fri–Mon was short. She doesn't know why it was changed and wasn't consulted.

**Policy excerpts (Harbor Point Code of Conduct)**

> 4.1 Harassment includes unwelcome physical contact and comments of a sexual nature. A single serious incident may violate this policy.
> 4.6 Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith is prohibited, including adverse changes to schedule or duties.
> 9.2 Findings are made on a preponderance of the evidence (more likely than not). Each allegation is labelled Substantiated, Not Substantiated or Inconclusive.

**Deliver** a report in markdown with these headed sections: Summary of findings; Scope and method; Allegations; Evidence and credibility assessment; Findings (one per allegation: the Sept 10 remark, the Sept 18 conduct, and retaliation via the schedule change), each with its label and reasoning; Recommended actions, including immediate remedies for the complainant; Open items. Use neutral, factual language that would stand up if read by an outside agency. Keep it under about 1,200 words.

## A strong answer

- Notices that Roe's denial that he attended the dinner is contradicted by his own expense receipt for that dinner and by his Slack message the next morning. Treats this as damaging to his credibility overall, not only on that one point.
- Marks the Sept 18 conduct Substantiated, citing Stiles's eyewitness account, the receipt and the Slack DM. Labels the Sept 10 remark with a reasoned position, for example Substantiated because Major told Stiles about it at the time and Roe's credibility is weakened, or Inconclusive with the reasons given.
- Spots the retaliation sequence: the draft moved three people before the complaint, but Roe cut it to Major alone about 17 hours after HR notified him. Marks retaliation Substantiated or explains carefully why not, and doesn't accept the "operational call" explanation without testing it against seniority records.
- Gives Miles's unnamed second-hand account little or no weight, and says why.
- Recommends concrete remedies: reverse Major's schedule change, remove Roe from supervising her or from any scheduling decisions about her, discipline proportionate to the findings, and follow-up check-ins. Lists open items such as verifying seniority records and checking for further retaliation.
