## Prompt

You are the in-house designer at Tidewater Community Credit Union, a fictional regional credit union. Marketing is launching "Easy Checking", a new checking account, and needs two launch graphics: an Instagram feed post (1080 × 1080) and a LinkedIn link image (1200 × 627).

**Brief from the marketing lead, Jane Doe**

> Headline: "No fees. Ever." Big and bold. Show a happy young family at the kitchen table. Put the CTA "Open in 5 minutes" and our logo. We want the 2.25% APY front and centre, because nobody else locally beats it. Keep it fun: our audience is first-time account holders, 22–35.

**Product sheet (approved by Compliance)**

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Monthly maintenance fee | $0 |
| Minimum opening deposit | $25 |
| Paper statements | $2 per month (e-statements free) |
| Out-of-network ATM | $2.50 per withdrawal; 4 network ATM fee refunds per month |
| Overdraft fee | $0; transactions declined instead |
| Dividend rate | 2.25% APY on balances up to $5,000; 0.10% APY above $5,000 |
| Account opening | Online in about 5 minutes with a valid ID |

**Compliance rules for ads (excerpt)**

> Any ad that states a rate must say "APY" and give the balance tiers or a "rate applies to balances up to…" line, and must include "APY = Annual Percentage Yield. Rates may change after account opening." Every ad must show "Federally insured by NCUA" with the insurance logo, no smaller than the minimum size in the brand guide. Absolute claims ("never", "ever", "no fees") are allowed only if literally true for the product.

**Brand guide (excerpt)**

- Colours: Tidewater Navy #0B3B5C, Sea Glass #6FC3B2, Sand #F4EBDD, white.
- Type: one sans-serif family, two weights. Headline at least 64 px on 1080 px artboards.
- Logo: minimum width 160 px on social, clear space equal to the height of the "T".
- The NCUA insurance logo: minimum width 120 px on social.
- Photography: real members, natural light. No stock clichés such as piggy banks or money rain.

**Deliver** (at most ~1,200 words of prose; the SVG may run longer):

1. Final copy for both formats: headline, sub-line, CTA and all required disclosure text. If you change the brief's headline, explain why and offer two alternatives Jane could choose from.
2. Layout and visual direction for each format: grid, hierarchy, where the photo, rate, logo and disclosures sit, and how the layout adapts from square to landscape.
3. An inline SVG mock-up for each format, in code blocks, at the true pixel dimensions. Use labelled placeholder rectangles for the logos and the photo, and make all copy legible at those sizes.
4. A short note to Jane on the claims you could not use, and why.

## A strong answer

- Rejects "No fees. Ever." because paper statements ($2) and out-of-network ATM ($2.50) fees exist, and proposes accurate alternatives such as "No monthly fees" or "$0 monthly fee, $0 overdraft".
- Shows the 2.25% APY with the "on balances up to $5,000" tier and the full APY disclosure. Drops or softens "nobody else locally beats it", since it is an unsupported comparative claim.
- Includes "Federally insured by NCUA" and the insurance logo at 120 px or wider, the logo at 160 px or wider with clear space, and a headline of at least 64 px on the 1080 px artboard.
- Provides two valid SVGs at 1080 × 1080 and 1200 × 627 with a clear hierarchy (headline, rate, CTA, disclosures) and brand colours only. The landscape version is recomposed, not just scaled down.
- The photo direction follows the guide (real members, natural light) while keeping the brief's family idea, and the copy sounds right for 22–35-year-old first-time account holders.
