## Prompt

You are the founder of ShiftBay, a fictional staff-scheduling product for outpatient clinics. You personally run sales for your top target accounts. Write the outreach for one of them.

**CRM record**

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Account | Riverbend Family Health (fictional), 14 outpatient clinics, about 420 clinical staff |
| Contact | Mary Major, VP of Clinical Operations, mary.major@riverbend.example.com |
| Source | Attended our webinar "Fixing last-minute shift gaps" on 3 September; asked a question about float-pool nurses |
| Current tool | Competitor scheduling suite; contract renews 30 November 2026, 90-day cancellation notice required |
| Known pain (from her webinar question) | "We burn hours every week filling call-outs by text message, and overtime is our fastest-growing cost." |
| Public info | Riverbend opened 3 new clinics this year; job board shows 11 open RN roles |
| Previous touch | None |

**What we can offer**

- Call-out filling: open shifts pushed to qualified, available staff by app; first to accept gets it, within credential and overtime rules.
- Float-pool management across sites.
- Implementation in about 6 weeks; data import from the competitor's export format.
- Discount policy: up to 15% for a multi-year contract at the founder's discretion. Anything above that needs CFO approval and has never been granted.

**Proof points (internal sheet)**

| Customer (fictional) | Clinics | Result after 6 months |
|---|---|---|
| Northfield Clinics | 9 | Overtime hours −40%; call-outs filled in a median of 22 min |
| Pinegate Health | 5 | Overtime hours −12% |
| Lakeview Medical | 12 | Overtime hours −15% |
| Other 3 customers | 4–8 each | Overtime hours −8% to −14% |
| All 6 customers | | Overtime hours −18% on average |

Northfield and Pinegate have agreed to be named and to take reference calls. The others have not.

**Request from your sales advisor, John Stiles:** "Open with the 40% overtime cut, offer 30% off if she signs by Friday, and ask for a demo next week."

**Deliver** (at most ~1,200 words):

1. A three-email sequence to Mary: subject line, body, and planned send day for each. The first email goes out on Tuesday, 16 September 2025.
2. A LinkedIn connection note of 300 characters or fewer.
3. A short note to John explaining which parts of his advice you used or rejected, and why.

Keep each email under 150 words, written as a founder rather than a marketing team, with one clear call to action each.

## A strong answer

- Does not present 40% as the typical result. It either uses the 18% average or cites Northfield's 40% clearly as one named customer, and names only Northfield and Pinegate.
- Rejects the 30% sign-by-Friday offer, since it breaks the 15% policy, is not credible for a 14-clinic healthcare buyer, and is premature with no meeting held. Any discount is kept for later and within policy.
- Uses the timing sensibly: the competitor contract renews 30 November 2026 with 90 days' notice (by about 1 September 2026), so this is a long-cycle evaluation. The CTA is a short discovery or demo call, not an immediate signature.
- Personalizes to her webinar question (call-outs by text message, overtime, float pool) and Riverbend's growth (3 new clinics, 11 open RN roles), with each email adding new value rather than "just following up".
- Each email is under 150 words with one CTA and a sensible send day. The LinkedIn note is 300 characters or fewer, and the tone is a founder's, not a pushy marketing voice.
