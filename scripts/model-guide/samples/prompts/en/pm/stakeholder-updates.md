## Prompt

You are the PM for "Goals", a new savings feature in the Kestrel Pay mobile banking app. It is built with a partner bank, Larchmont Bank, which holds the deposits. You have a 15-minute slot at Thursday's steering committee: John Stiles (COO), Mary Major (CFO) and Richard Miles (Head of Compliance). Build the deck from the status inputs below. The team lead, Jane Doe, has rated the project **green overall**.

**Project facts**

- Planned public launch: **1 December 2026**. The project runs 10 months (February–November); 7 months have elapsed.
- Partner-bank rule (from the programme agreement): Larchmont Bank needs **4 weeks** to review the final compliance pack before any public launch.

**Milestones (from Jane's status report)**

| Milestone | Planned | Current forecast | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Closed beta | 15 Aug | 15 Aug (done) | Green |
| Ledger integration with Larchmont | 30 Sep | 10 Oct | Green |
| Final compliance pack submitted to Larchmont | 31 Oct | 14 Nov | Green |
| Marketing launch assets | 15 Nov | 15 Nov | Green |
| Public launch | 1 Dec | 1 Dec | Green |

**Budget**

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Approved budget | $600,000 |
| Spent to date (7 months) | $455,000 |
| Current monthly run-rate | $65,000 |
| Forecast at completion (per status report) | $590,000 |

**Beta results (2,400 users, 6 weeks)**

| Metric | Target | Actual |
|---|---|---|
| Users who created a savings goal | 40% | 31% |
| Average first deposit | $75 | $86 |
| Week-4 retention of goal creators | 60% | 64% |
| Support tickets per 1,000 users | ≤15 | 22 |

Top beta ticket themes: "didn't understand the round-up setting" (41% of tickets) and "deposit took 2 days to show" (27%).

**Deliver** (markdown, at most ~1,200 words):

1. A deck of 6–8 slides, written slide by slide (`### Slide n: title`, 3–5 bullets each, and a one-line speaker note). It should open with the bottom line, give an honest RAG status, and end with a specific decision you need from the committee, with options and your recommendation.
2. A cover email (at most 120 words) to send with the deck.
3. A note (at most 100 words) to Jane Doe explaining any status you changed and why. Keep it constructive.

## A strong answer

- Recomputes the forecast at completion as about $650,000 ($455,000 + 3 remaining months × $65,000). Flags that this conflicts with the reported $590,000 and is roughly $50,000 (about 8%) over budget.
- Catches the launch conflict: the compliance pack is submitted 14 Nov, and with Larchmont's 4-week review the earliest launch is about 12 Dec, after the 1 Dec date. The compliance and launch milestones should not be green.
- Reports the beta honestly: goal creation at 31% is below the 40% target and tickets at 22 per 1,000 exceed the limit of 15. Positives (deposit size, retention) are presented without spin, and the ticket themes are linked to fixes.
- Ends with a clear decision request with options (for example, move the launch to mid-December or January, or a soft launch to a limited audience if Larchmont allows it, plus budget approval or descoping) and a justified recommendation.
- Keeps to 6–8 slides with a speaker note on each. The deck reads at executive level with the bottom line first, and the note to Jane is factual and non-blaming.
