## Prompt

You are the in-house recruiter at Northgale Renewables, which operates onshore wind farms. The site manager, Richard Roe, sent the draft posting below for a **Wind Turbine Technician I** at the Cedar Ridge Wind Farm. Rewrite it so it is accurate, inclusive and compliant. Then produce a square social-media recruiting graphic for it. The posting will run in a state that requires a good-faith pay range in every job ad.

**Draft from Richard Roe**

> **Wind Tech Rockstar Wanted!**
> We're looking for a young, energetic guy to join our crew at Cedar Ridge. Recent graduates are ideal. You'll climb turbines, troubleshoot and keep the blades spinning.
> **Requirements:** Bachelor's degree in engineering. Native English speaker. 2+ years of wind experience. Must be able to climb 80 m towers and lift 50 lb. Clean driving record.
> **Pay:** $28–34/hour plus overtime.
> Work hard, play hard. Apply now!

**HR leveling guide, Technician I**

- No degree required. A technical certificate or equivalent hands-on experience (electrical, mechanical, military) is preferred.
- GWO Basic Safety Training is required, or must be completed within 90 days of hire (paid by Northgale).
- 0–2 years of wind experience. Training is provided.
- Communication requirement: able to follow and give safety-critical instructions in English over radio.

**Compensation sheet (approved this quarter)**

| Role | Site | Hourly range |
|---|---|---|
| Wind Turbine Technician I | Cedar Ridge | $31–38 |
| Wind Turbine Technician II | Cedar Ridge | $37–45 |

**Job facts**

- Rotating 4×10 schedule. Overtime during the maintenance season (April–October).
- Physical demands: climbing ladders in 80 m towers, working at height in a harness, lifting up to 50 lb, outdoor work in heat and cold.
- A valid driver's licence is required (the site is 40 minutes from town and has company trucks).
- Benefits: medical, dental, 401(k) match up to 4%, paid GWO training, and a tool allowance.
- Apply at careers.example.com/cedar-ridge-tech1. Contact: recruiting@example.com.

**Deliver** (markdown, at most ~1,200 words excluding the SVG):

1. The revised job posting, at most 450 words.
2. A change log table: original text, revised text, and the reason for each change (legal, accuracy or inclusivity).
3. A 1080×1080 social recruiting graphic:
   - all the on-image copy
   - the layout, described by zone
   - visual direction (colour, imagery, typography, accessibility)
   - an inline SVG mock-up in a code block (`viewBox="0 0 1080 1080"`, no external assets)

## A strong answer

- Removes the age-coded and gendered language ("young, energetic guy", "recent graduates") and replaces "native English speaker" (national-origin risk) with the job-related requirement: safety-critical communication in English over radio.
- Uses the approved $31–38/hour range, and flags that the draft's $28–34 doesn't match the compensation sheet and would break the pay-transparency rule.
- Aligns the requirements with the leveling guide: no bachelor's degree, 0–2 years of wind experience with training provided, and GWO training within 90 days paid by Northgale. The "2+ years" requirement is gone, because it belongs to the Technician II level.
- Keeps the genuine physical, height and driving requirements, stated plainly, and adds an equal-opportunity and reasonable-accommodation statement. Drops "rockstar" and "work hard, play hard".
- The SVG is valid and self-contained at 1080×1080. It shows the role, the location, the $31–38/hour range, one or two benefits and the apply URL, with legible type and adequate contrast, and it is consistent with the revised posting.
