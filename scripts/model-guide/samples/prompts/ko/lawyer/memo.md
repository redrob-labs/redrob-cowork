## A strong answer

- Distinguishes the §3.14 representation claim under 8.2(a), whose contractual survival period expired April 30, 2024 (a valid shortening), from the independent Pre-Closing Tax covenant in 8.2(c). Answers the 민법 §184② argument: 8.6 sets a contractual survival period for the covenant rather than extending a running limitation period; the 5-year 상사시효 (상법 §64) for the indemnity claim runs only from when the claim could be exercised (§166①), i.e. from the Aug 4, 2025 assessment, so it is nowhere near expiry. Either way the claim made on Nov 3, 2025 is within the 7-year outer limit (April 30, 2028). Concludes the claim is timely under 8.2(c).
- Addresses the notice defense. Notice came 91 days after the August 4 assessment, so the 30-day deadline was missed. Under 8.7 that defeats the claim only to the extent the sellers were actually prejudiced, and the memo says what prejudice they might argue, notably that the 90-day 심판청구 window under 국세기본법 §68① ran out around Nov 3 without their input, and flags confirming whether Tessaline filed an appeal.
- Computes the recoverable amount with the closing-of-the-books allocation: ₩2억 6천만 + ₩2억 9천만 + ₩1억 1,200만 = ₩6억 6,200만 (not the pro-rata ₩1억 667만 for January–April). Excludes the post-closing May–December 2021 and 2022 tax, which is Juniper's own liability.
- Notes that the 8.2(c) claim isn't subject to the Deductible, the Escrow cap or the released escrow. Splits the liability severally per 8.4: ₩4억 6,340만 (Founders LLC) and ₩1억 9,860만 (이영희).
- Recommends withdrawing or amending the ₩11억 2천만 notice so it claims under 8.2(c) for ₩6억 6,200만, plus any related 가산세 or interest if documented and allocable, and preserving any available challenge to the assessment.
